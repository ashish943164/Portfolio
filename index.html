Object Detection and Tracking for Smart Surveillance with Automated Email Alerts

Ashish Kumar and Prof. Dr. Renu Bagoria

This research presents an AI-based surveillance system designed to detect people and weapons in video streams, track detected objects and send automated email alerts with captured snapshots. It integrates YOLOv8, DeepSORT, Python, OpenCV and the Ultralytics framework for video analysis and real-time monitoring.
